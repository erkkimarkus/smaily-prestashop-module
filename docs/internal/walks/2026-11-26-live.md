# Live walk: the owner's live shop against the real Smaily, engine and report receiver — 2026-11-26

Internal record. On the owner's request a worker walked the first-release capabilities in the
owner's live test shop (Compose project `erkkilive`, PrestaShop 9.2.0, debug mode off, English and
Estonian, theme hummingbird, one shop) against the **real** services: the owner's Smaily test
account, the Campaign Intelligence engine at `intelligence.smaily.com` and the installation-report
receiver at `subscription.smaily.com`. Nothing in the module was changed. Every shopper and address
is invented (`walk.*@example.com`); no credential, key, token or account identifier is recorded.

- **Commit walked:** `b68643c` at the start (17:22 UTC). `2fe0ffc` (the RSS URL builder's language)
  was merged into `v3` during the walk; the shop's cache was cleared and every admin screen walked
  again in both languages on `2fe0ffc` (section 9).
- **How:** the merchant's part in the admin screens and the shopper's part in the storefront, in a
  headless Chromium (playwright-core) whose requests stayed on the shop; the background work through
  the shop's own cron trigger (`tiksur`, the tick URL every minute) — no job was made due by hand.
  The store side was read by SQL that selects no credential column. What Smaily holds for a walk
  address was read through the module's own stored account in a one-off read-only script inside the
  shop (one `GET contact` per invented address, printing only known / `is_unsubscribed` /
  `language`); the engine's recommendations were read the same way (the number of slots only).
- **Result:** every capability that the owner's settings switch on works against the real services.
  **One defect** (section 7.3): a guest who arrives from an email link and checks out leaves a
  failed Log row, and the Dashboard turns to "Needs attention". Waiting: the engine answers no
  recommendations yet; a change made in Smaily that flows into the store needs the owner's hand.

### 7.3 Defect: a guest's identity merge after an email link fails for good

Reproduction (live engine, tracking confirmed, "Subscribers only"): open a product's friendly URL
with `?smaily_rec=<uuid>&smaily_ctx=newsletter&smaily_vt=vt_<letters>`, add to cart, check out as a
guest with the newsletter box empty. At the personal-information step an `engine.identity_merge`
row is queued; the engine answers 404 and the row fails at once with `engine_customer_not_sent`,
"will NOT retry on its own". The store never sends a guest as a customer
(`CustomerScope::includes()`), so Retry cannot help either. The Log shows the row as Failed with the
sentence "… so the login was not linked to them …" (there was no login), and the Dashboard turns to
"NEEDS ATTENTION — 1 event failed in the last 24 hours". Every guest who orders after an email
click would do the same. The mock walks did not show it. A cross-connector question (CLAUDE.md:
one connector never changes a behaviour rule alone): should a guest's merge be skipped, or the guest
be sent? Severity: medium (a false alarm the merchant cannot clear).

## 10. Observations

- PrestaShop's redirect drops the query of `/en/?…` (the default language's home goes to `/`) and of
  `index.php?id_product=…&…` (to the friendly URL). A link that carries `smaily_rec` / `smaily_vt`
  that way loses its attribution; a friendly product URL and `/?…` keep it. Also a search address
  `index.php?controller=search&s=…` loses its words. PrestaShop's behaviour, not the module's; it
  matters for the links the engine and Smaily build.
- The engine had no recommendations for the store about 45 minutes after its history import:
  re-check after the engine's nightly run.
