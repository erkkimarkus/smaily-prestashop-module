OverviewÜlevaade

Smaily Connect links your PrestaShop store to Smaily email marketing and, optionally, to Smaily Campaign Intelligence for personalized product recommendations. Smaily Connect ühendab sinu PrestaShopi poe Smaily e-turundusega ja soovi korral Smaily Campaign Intelligence'iga, mis pakub personaalseid tootesoovitusi.

What is Smaily Connect?Mis on Smaily Connect?

Smaily Connect for PrestaShop is a module for PrestaShop 8 and 9. It links your store to your own Smaily email-marketing account and, optionally, to Smaily Campaign Intelligence — Smaily's recommendation engine for personalized products and purchase-based automations. It is the next version of the module Smaily for PrestaShop: a store on version 2.x upgrades in place and keeps its settings (see Upgrading from 2.x).

It does four things:

  • Syncs contacts — your newsletter subscribers (or, if you choose, every customer) reach your Smaily contact list, and an unsubscribe in Smaily comes back to PrestaShop.
  • Triggers automations — store events (welcome, first order, abandoned cart) start your Smaily workflows, with a discount code if you want one.
  • Powers recommendations — when you connect Campaign Intelligence, your catalog, customers and orders (and, once you confirm your cookie banner, browsing) feed personalized recommendations in your emails and your store, and engine-run automations.
  • Serves a product RSS feed — for the RSS block of the Smaily template editor.

Your data stays yours. Contacts live in your Smaily account; the module only connects the two systems.

Smaily Connect for PrestaShop on moodul PrestaShopi versioonidele 8 ja 9. See ühendab sinu poe sinu enda Smaily e-turunduse kontoga ja soovi korral Smaily Campaign Intelligence'iga — Smaily soovitusmootoriga, mis pakub personaalseid tooteid ja ostupõhiseid automaatikaid. See on mooduli Smaily for PrestaShop järgmine versioon: versiooni 2.x pood uueneb kohapeal ja jätab oma seaded alles (vt Uuendamine versioonilt 2.x).

See teeb nelja asja:

  • Sünkroonib kontakte — sinu uudiskirja tellijad (või soovi korral kõik kliendid) jõuavad sinu Smaily kontaktide nimekirja ning Smailys tehtud loobumine jõuab tagasi PrestaShopi.
  • Käivitab automaatikaid — poe sündmused (tervitus, esimene tellimus, hüljatud ostukorv) käivitavad sinu Smaily töövood, soovi korral koos sooduskoodiga.
  • Toidab soovitusi — kui ühendad Campaign Intelligence'i, toidavad sinu kataloog, kliendid ja tellimused (ning pärast küpsiste bänneri kinnitamist ka sirvimine) personaalseid soovitusi sinu e-kirjades ja poes ning mootoris töötavaid automaatikaid.
  • Pakub toodete RSS-voogu — Smaily mallide redaktori RSS-ploki jaoks.

Sinu andmed jäävad sinu omaks. Kontaktid asuvad sinu Smaily kontol; moodul ainult ühendab kaks süsteemi.

How the pieces fit togetherKuidas osad kokku sobivad

There are three systems:

  • PrestaShop — your store: customers, newsletter subscribers, orders, products and storefront activity.
  • Smaily — your email-marketing account, where contacts and automation workflows live (yourcompany.sendsmaily.net).
  • Campaign Intelligence — Smaily's recommendation engine. Optional, connected with a one-time setup link from Smaily.

Smaily Connect is the bridge. Store events reach the module; the module queues contacts and automation triggers for Smaily and — if connected — catalog, customer and order data for Campaign Intelligence. The module's background tasks send the queue, normally within a minute.

Under the hood

PrestaShop has no scheduler of its own. The background tasks run when a cron line on your server calls one address of your store every minute, and, without one, when visitors open pages of your store (see Background tasks). A delivery that fails is retried on its own, so nothing is lost when Smaily or Campaign Intelligence is briefly out of reach. Every delivery shows in Customers > Smaily Connect > Log.

Süsteeme on kolm:

  • PrestaShop — sinu pood: kliendid, uudiskirja tellijad, tellimused, tooted ja tegevus poes.
  • Smaily — sinu e-turunduse konto, kus asuvad kontaktid ja automaatikate töövood (sinufirma.sendsmaily.net).
  • Campaign Intelligence — Smaily soovitusmootor. Valikuline, ühendatakse Smailylt saadud ühekordse seadistuslingiga.

Smaily Connect on sild. Poe sündmused jõuavad moodulini; moodul paneb kontaktid ja automaatikate päästikud Smaily jaoks järjekorda ning — kui ühendatud — kataloogi-, kliendi- ja tellimusandmed Campaign Intelligence'i jaoks. Järjekorra saadavad välja mooduli taustatööd, tavaliselt minuti jooksul.

Kapoti all

PrestaShopil pole oma ajastajat. Taustatööd käivituvad, kui sinu serveris olev cron-rida kutsub iga minut välja sinu poe ühe aadressi, ja kui seda pole, siis siis, kui külastajad avavad sinu poe lehti (vt Taustatööd). Ebaõnnestunud saadetist proovitakse ise uuesti, nii et kui Smaily või Campaign Intelligence on korraks kättesaamatu, ei lähe midagi kaduma. Iga saadetis on näha menüüs Kliendid > Smaily Connect > Logi.

Before you startEnne alustamist

You need:

  • A PrestaShop store on a supported version (below). The back office shows the PrestaShop version in Advanced Parameters > Information, with the PHP version.
  • A way to run the background tasks: best a cron line on your server, or a cron service, that calls your store every minute (see Adding the cron line). Without one, the tasks run only while visitors open pages of your store.
  • A Smaily account with API access. Create an API user in Smaily under settings (gear icon) > Integrations — you'll need the subdomain, the API username and the API password. The account's package must include API access.
  • (Optional) A Campaign Intelligence setup link — a one-time link on https://intelligence.smaily.com/…, provided by Smaily if you use the recommendation add-on.
  • (With Campaign Intelligence) Storefront addresses your firewall lets through. The shopper's browser posts to the module's addresses on your store's own domain: /module/smailyforprestashop/land (an email-link click) and /module/smailyforprestashop/relay (browse events), and asks /module/smailyforprestashop/recommendations for the recommendations block. A web application firewall, a CDN rule or a security module must let them through. They need no login and carry no name or email address.

Getting startedAlustamine

Install the module, then work through the five-step initial setup in the back office. Paigalda moodul ja läbi halduses viiesammuline algseadistus.

Install the moduleMooduli paigaldus

  1. Download smailyforprestashop.zip of the latest release from Smaily's GitHub repository.
  2. In the back office, open Modules > Module Manager, click Upload a module and drop the zip file there. PrestaShop installs the module.
  3. Open Customers > Smaily Connect. A new store starts in the Initial setup.

You can also extract the zip into the modules/ folder of your store, so that the folder modules/smailyforprestashop holds the module, and click Install next to it in Modules > Module Manager.

Upgrading from 2.x?

Upload the new zip over the old module. Do not uninstall the old version first: uninstalling removes every setting of the module, the cron tokens and the record of reminded carts. See Upgrading from 2.x.

  1. Laadi uusima väljalaske fail smailyforprestashop.zip alla Smaily GitHubi hoidlast.
  2. Ava halduses Moodulid > Moodulihaldur (Modules > Module Manager), klõpsa Laadi moodul üles (Upload a module) ja lohista ZIP-fail sinna. PrestaShop paigaldab mooduli.
  3. Ava Kliendid > Smaily Connect. Uus pood alustab algseadistusega.

Võid ZIP-i ka lahti pakkida oma poe kausta modules/, nii et moodul on kaustas modules/smailyforprestashop, ja klõpsata menüüs Moodulid > Moodulihaldur selle kõrval nuppu Paigalda (Install).

Uuendad versioonilt 2.x?

Laadi uus ZIP üles vana mooduli peale. Ära eemalda vana versiooni enne: eemaldamine kustutab kõik mooduli seaded, cron-võtmed ja meeldetuletatud ostukorvide kirje. Vt Uuendamine versioonilt 2.x.

If your store has several shopsKui sinu paigalduses on mitu poodi

On an installation with more than one PrestaShop shop (multistore), the initial setup first asks How are your shops run?:

  • One store in several versions — the shops are country or language versions of one business, on one Smaily account. You set the store up once; a shop differs only where you give it its own settings.
  • Separate stores — each shop is a business of its own, with its own Smaily account, contacts and automations. Pick a shop in the shop selector at the top of the page and set each shop up on its own, as a single store.

An installation with one shop is not asked, and sees none of this. In One store in several versions, the initial setup shows and saves the settings of all shops, whichever shop is selected; a shop's own settings are changed in Settings — see How your shops are run.

The shop selector applies to every open back-office tab. When the selected shop changes after a Smaily Connect page was opened, for example in another browser tab, the page saves nothing and asks you to reload it, so that a setting never lands on another shop than the one the page shows.

Kui paigalduses on rohkem kui üks PrestaShopi pood (multistore), küsib algseadistus kõigepealt Kuidas on sinu poed korraldatud?:

  • Üks pood mitmes versioonis — poed on ühe ettevõtte riigi- või keeleversioonid ühel Smaily kontol. Seadistad poe üks kord; pood erineb ainult seal, kus annad talle oma seaded.
  • Eraldi poed — iga pood on omaette ettevõte, oma Smaily konto, kontaktide ja automaatikatega. Vali lehe ülaosas poevalijast pood ja seadista iga pood eraldi, nagu üksikpood.

Ühe poega paigalduselt seda ei küsita ja ta ei näe sellest midagi. Valiku Üks pood mitmes versioonis korral näitab ja salvestab algseadistus kõigi poodide seadeid, olenemata valitud poest; poe enda seadeid muudetakse lehel Seaded — vt Kuidas poed on korraldatud.

Poevalik kehtib igas avatud halduse vahekaardis. Kui valitud pood muutub pärast Smaily Connecti lehe avamist, näiteks teises brauseri vahekaardis, ei salvesta leht midagi ja palub end uuesti laadida, et seade ei satuks kunagi teisele poele kui see, mida leht näitab.

Step 1 — Connect your Smaily accountSamm 1 — Ühenda oma Smaily konto

Open Customers > Smaily Connect > Initial setup. The step list shows the five steps; Back and Continue move between them, and each step saves when you continue. The step list opens a later step only while the Smaily connection of step 1 is in order — a passed Test connection, or a saved account that Smaily accepts; until then a click on a later step shows Test your Smaily connection to continue.

  1. Enter the Subdomain — if your Smaily URL is https://demo.sendsmaily.net, the subdomain is demo. Pasting the full URL also works.
  2. Enter the API username and API password of your Smaily API user — to create one, log in to Smaily, open the settings (the gear icon) > Integrations and create a new API user and password there. The password is stored encrypted, and a saved password is never shown again.
  3. Press Test connection. It checks the credentials as typed, without saving.
  4. If your store speaks several languages, pick under How is Smaily organized for your store languages? how they use Smaily — see Multilingual stores. A single-language store does not see this.
  5. Press Continue. It waits until Test connection passes: Test your Smaily connection to continue. With Per-language Smaily accounts, every account you fill in is tested. A change to the subdomain, username or password asks for the test again and clears the connection status.

A saved account counts as tested once Smaily accepts it when the page opens, so a saved password is not typed again. Saving the connection sends no contacts yet: contact sync starts when step 2 is saved.

Ava Kliendid > Smaily Connect > Algseadistus. Sammude loend näitab viit sammu; Tagasi ja Jätka liiguvad nende vahel ning iga samm salvestub, kui jätkad. Sammude loend avab hilisema sammu alles siis, kui sammu 1 Smaily ühendus on korras — Testi ühendust õnnestus või Smaily võtab salvestatud konto vastu; seni näitab klõps hilisemal sammul teadet Jätkamiseks testi oma Smaily ühendust.

  1. Sisesta Alamdomeen — kui sinu Smaily URL on https://demo.sendsmaily.net, on alamdomeen demo. Sobib ka täieliku URL-i kleepimine.
  2. Sisesta oma Smaily API kasutaja API kasutajanimi ja API parool — selle loomiseks logi Smailysse sisse, ava seaded (hammasratta ikoon) > Liidestused ja loo seal uus API-kasutaja ja parool. Parool salvestatakse krüpteeritult ja salvestatud parooli enam kunagi ei näidata.
  3. Vajuta Testi ühendust. See kontrollib sisestatud kasutajaandmeid ilma salvestamata.
  4. Kui sinu pood räägib mitut keelt, vali küsimuse Kuidas on Smaily sinu poe keelte jaoks korraldatud? all, kuidas keeled Smailyt kasutavad — vt Mitmekeelsed poed. Ühekeelne pood seda ei näe.
  5. Vajuta Jätka. See ootab, kuni Testi ühendust õnnestub: Jätkamiseks testi oma Smaily ühendust. Valiku Keelepõhised Smaily kontod korral testitakse iga täidetud konto. Alamdomeeni, kasutajanime või parooli muutmine küsib testi uuesti ja peidab ühenduse oleku.

Salvestatud konto loetakse testituks, kui Smaily selle lehe avamisel vastu võtab, nii et salvestatud parooli uuesti ei trükita. Ühenduse salvestamine ei saada veel ühtegi kontakti: kontaktide sünkroonimine algab sammu 2 salvestamisel.

Step 2 — ContactsSamm 2 — Kontaktid

Who should be synced to Smaily? This is a lawful-basis choice: pick the mode that matches how you have asked your customers for permission.

  • Subscribers only (consent) — Recommended. Only customers who opted into the newsletter are synced, and unsubscribes in Smaily come back to PrestaShop.
  • All customers (legitimate interest) — every registered customer is synced (soft opt-in).
  • Checkout opt-in only — nobody is synced automatically, only shoppers who tick the newsletter checkbox at checkout.

Then pick Include guests and the Extra fields to sync with each contact, and press Continue. Contacts start syncing to Smaily when you save this step. Until then nothing goes to Smaily. Saving sends none of the contacts you already have: they reach Smaily only when you press Start import under Initial contact import — see What gets imported. Each option is explained under Settings › Contacts.

Another integration already syncs subscribers?

If another module or service already sends your newsletter subscribers or opt-outs to Smaily, switch it off once Smaily Connect syncs contacts — after this step is saved with Sync contacts to Smaily ticked. Not before, so no opt-out is missed in between.

Keda Smailysse sünkroonida? See on õigusliku aluse valik: vali režiim, mis vastab sellele, kuidas oled klientidelt luba küsinud.

  • Ainult turunduskirjade tellijad (nõusolekuga) — Soovitatav. Sünkroonitakse ainult kliendid, kes on uudiskirjaga liitunud, ning Smailys tehtud loobumised jõuavad tagasi PrestaShopi.
  • Kõik kliendid (õigustatud huvi) — sünkroonitakse iga registreeritud klient (pehme nõusolek).
  • Ainult kassas turunduskirjade nõusoleku andnud kontaktid — kedagi ei sünkroonita automaatselt, ainult ostjad, kes märgivad kassas uudiskirja märkeruudu.

Seejärel vali Kaasa külalised ja Lisaväljad, mis sünkroonitakse iga kontaktiga ning vajuta Jätka. Kontaktide sünkroonimine Smailysse algab, kui salvestad selle sammu. Seni ei saadeta Smailysse midagi. Salvestamine ei saada ühtegi juba olemasolevat kontakti: need jõuavad Smailysse alles siis, kui vajutad jaotises Esmane kontaktide import nuppu Alusta importi — vt Mida imporditakse. Iga valikut selgitab jaotis Seaded › Kontaktid.

Kas mõni teine lõiming juba sünkroonib tellijaid?

Kui mõni teine moodul või teenus saadab juba sinu uudiskirja tellijaid või loobumisi Smailysse, lülita see välja siis, kui Smaily Connect kontakte sünkroonib — pärast selle sammu salvestamist märgitud valikuga Sünkrooni kontaktid Smailysse. Mitte varem, et vahepeal ükski loobumine kaduma ei läheks.

Step 3 — AutomationsSamm 3 — Automaatikad

Map store events to Smaily automations. For each event — Welcome, First order, Abandoned cart — tick Enabled and pick a Smaily Workflow. Abandoned cartHüljatud ostukorv

Wait (minutes) is how long a cart must be inactive before it counts as abandoned, from 10 to 1435 minutes (30 on a new store). A cart older than 24 hours is never reminded. The background tasks look for abandoned carts every 5 minutes.

  • A cart needs an email address: a signed-in customer's, or the one a shopper typed at checkout. A typed address is kept for that cart's reminder only; it is not sent to Smaily as a contact.
  • A cart gets one reminder, and an address gets at most one reminder in 24 hours, also for another cart.
  • A reminder that waits is withdrawn when the shopper orders in the meantime (Withdrawn in the Log).
  • The reminder follows the consent mode by the rule under Automations.

Every reminder carries the customer's first and last name and up to 10 products in the cart, with the legacy fields of version 2.x — see Abandoned cart fields. If another tool also sends abandoned-cart emails (for example another PrestaShop module such as Customer follow-up), switch those emails off there, so a shopper does not get two reminders for one cart.

Ooteaeg (minutites) on aeg, mille jooksul ostukorv peab olema tegevuseta, enne kui see loetakse hüljatuks, 10 kuni 1435 minutit (uues poes 30). Üle 24 tunni vanale ostukorvile meeldetuletust ei saadeta. Taustatööd otsivad hüljatud ostukorve iga 5 minuti järel.

  • Ostukorvil peab olema e-posti aadress: sisse loginud kliendi oma või see, mille ostja kassas sisestas. Sisestatud aadressi hoitakse ainult selle ostukorvi meeldetuletuse jaoks; seda ei saadeta Smailysse kontaktina.

  • Ootav meeldetuletus võetakse tagasi, kui ostja vahepeal tellib (logis Tagasi võetud).
  • Meeldetuletus järgib nõusolekurežiimi jaotises Automaatikad kirjeldatud reegli järgi.

Iga meeldetuletus sisaldab kliendi ees- ja perekonnanime ning kuni 10 ostukorvi toodet, versiooni 2.x väljadega — vt Hüljatud ostukorvi väljad. Kui hüljatud ostukorvi kirju saadab ka mõni muu tööriist (näiteks mõni teine PrestaShopi moodul, nagu Customer follow-up), lülita need kirjad seal välja, et ostja ei saaks ühe ostukorvi kohta kahte meeldetuletust.

IntelligenceIntelligence

Campaign Intelligence is Smaily's recommendation engine: it learns from your catalog, your customers and your orders, and puts products that fit each customer into your emails and your store. You connect it under Settings > Intelligence, or on step 4 of the initial setup.

  1. Ask Smaily for a setup link (Contact Smaily). Paste it into Setup URL or token from Smaily and press Connect. The link works once, and it is not stored. The tab then shows Connected with the name of your Campaign Intelligence store.
  2. A link that is not taken leaves the store unconnected, and the tab says why: the link was already used, it is not known, it belongs to another Smaily account, or Campaign Intelligence could not be reached. Ask Smaily for a new link.
  3. Connecting starts three imports, one after another: your Catalog, your Customers and your Orders. They run in the background from the next run of the background tasks.   6. Disconnect ends the connection, after a question — see How do I disconnect Campaign Intelligence?.

Browse tracking and your cookie bannerSirvimise jälgimine ja sinu küpsiste bänner

With Campaign Intelligence connected, the store can tell it which products, categories and searches a visitor looks at and what goes into the cart, so that the recommendations fit the visitor. The events go through your own store's server; no key or password of your store is ever in a page.

Browse tracking and the recommendations block are off until you confirm that your store's cookie banner covers them, on the card Storefront browse tracking and recommendations of Settings > Intelligence: tick I confirm that my store’s cookie banner lets a visitor choose whether to allow Smaily to track their browsing. and save. PrestaShop has no cookie-consent setting that a module can read, so Smaily Connect cannot see what a visitor answered to your banner. When you switch this on, every visitor of this shop is treated as having agreed to Smaily tracking and recommendations: you are responsible that your cookie banner asks for it. A new store and a store upgraded from 2.x start with this off.

The confirmation switches on, for that shop:

  • browse tracking: the product, category and search views and the cart changes;
  • the recommendations block (see Recommendations on your store's pages);
  • the link between a shopper and their browsing: when a customer signs in or registers, or a guest fills in their details at checkout, the store gives the browser the visitor token smaily_rec_uid. One rule holds for customers and guests: your confirmation decides, and the newsletter box plays no part in it. A customer who signs in or registers is also sent to Campaign Intelligence at that moment; a guest reaches it with their order.

Who is synced to Smaily as a contact does not change with this switch: that follows the consent mode in Settings > Contacts. A customer who switched personalization off is not linked (see Personalization opt-out).

For your developer

If your theme can read the banner's answer, it can give the module each visitor's own choice: define window.smailyConnect.consentOverride(), returning true only for a visitor who agreed. Where it is defined, its answer decides, whatever the setting says. When the answer changes on an open page, fire the event smaily:consent-changed on document.

Order attributionTellimuse seos e-kirja või tootesoovitusega

When a shopper arrives from a link in one of your Smaily emails, or clicks a product in the recommendations block, the store records the click in the cookies smaily_rec_id and smaily_rec_ctx, and an email link also brings the visitor token smaily_rec_uid. An order placed within 30 days carries the click, so Campaign Intelligence can credit the sale to the email or the recommendation. These landing cookies are set without the consent switch, as in every Smaily store connector: they record that the visitor came from your own email. Without consent nothing else is stored and no browsing is sent.

While a visitor has a cart, the store also keeps these values with that cart, by the same rules. An order that a payment provider confirms from its own server, without the visitor's browser, is then credited like any other order. The store deletes its copy when the order exists, or a day after the visitor's last visit with that cart.

Kui ostja tuleb mõne sinu Smaily e-kirja lingi kaudu või klõpsab soovituste plokis tootel, salvestab pood klõpsu küpsistesse smaily_rec_id ja smaily_rec_ctx ning e-kirja link toob kaasa ka külastajatunnuse smaily_rec_uid. 30 päeva jooksul esitatud tellimus kannab klõpsu kaasas, nii et Campaign Intelligence saab müügi omistada e-kirjale või soovitusele. Need saabumisküpsised seatakse ilma nõusolekulülitita, nagu igas Smaily poeühenduses: need märgivad, et külastaja tuli sinu enda e-kirjast. Ilma nõusolekuta ei salvestata midagi muud ega saadeta sirvimist.

Kuni külastajal on ostukorv, hoiab pood neid väärtusi samade reeglite järgi ka selle ostukorvi juures. Tellimus, mille makseteenuse pakkuja kinnitab oma serverist ilma külastaja brauserita, omistatakse siis nagu iga teine tellimus. Pood kustutab oma koopia, kui tellimus on olemas, või päev pärast külastaja viimast külastust selle ostukorviga.

Campaign Intelligence automationsCampaign Intelligence'i automaatikad

Under the store automations, Settings > Automations has the card Campaign Intelligence Automations. It lists the automated emails that Campaign Intelligence sends itself, at the moment it decides for each customer, for example a reminder when a product is about to run out. The list comes from Campaign Intelligence each time you open the tab, with the names and descriptions it gives. The card is not part of the initial setup, and it has its own Save Intelligence Automations. A store that is not connected sees Connect Campaign Intelligence to set up these automations instead.

  1. Create a workflow in Smaily for each automation you want, with the form submitted trigger, in the Smaily account that your Campaign Intelligence connection belongs to.
  2. Tick Enabled on the automation. Its settings open:
    • Test mode: while it is ticked, only the test addresses get the email. A newly enabled automation is in test mode.
    • Smaily Workflow: the workflow the automation starts. An enabled automation needs one.
    • Cooldown (days): the shortest time between two emails of this automation to one customer, from 1 to 365 days.
    • Daily Cap: the most emails a day, from 1 to 100000. Empty means no cap.
    • Test Emails: up to 50 addresses, separated by commas, that get the emails in test mode.
  3. Press Save Intelligence Automations. The card then shows the state Campaign Intelligence holds for each automation: Off, Test mode or Active.

Every automation is off until you tick Enabled: nothing reaches real customers without your own action. To send to real customers, untick Test mode and save; Smaily switches real sends on after you confirm, and until then the automation stays in Test mode. When the list cannot be loaded, the card says Could not load the automation catalog: nothing can be saved then, and your saved automations are unchanged.

What is sent after the importsMida saadetakse pärast importi

A connected store sends its changes with the next run of the background tasks, usually within a minute or two. Each delivery is a row of the Log with the Source Campaign Intelligence. A store without a connection sends nothing.

  • Products. A product is sent when you save it, when its stock changes, for example with an order, and when one of its prices changes. A product with combinations is sent as one row per combination. A product that is switched off or not visible is sent as out of stock, so that it is no longer recommended. A deleted product is removed from Campaign Intelligence.
  • Customers. A customer is sent when the account is created or changed and when the customer orders, but only a customer that your contact sync sends to Smaily, or a registered customer with an order in the last 60 months. The email address, the name, the language, and the country and phone number of the customer's first address are sent; no street address, birthday or note.
  • Orders. An order is sent when it is placed, and again each time its status changes, it is edited or a product of it is returned, with its lines, amounts and status, in the shop's default currency.

While Campaign Intelligence does not accept the connection, the changes wait and are sent once it accepts the connection again.

The nightly product listÖine tootenimekiri

Once a night, from 03:30, the store sends Campaign Intelligence the list of its products with whether each is in stock. Campaign Intelligence stops recommending a product that the list leaves out, so a deletion or a stock change that did not arrive is put right within a night.

  • A store with more than 50,000 product rows sends no list, and the Log says so each night. The regular product changes still go.
  • Where two shops of the installation are connected to the same Campaign Intelligence store, the list is not sent for them, and the Dashboard says so: a deleted product or a stock change reaches Campaign Intelligence only with the product's next change.
  • When no list has gone out for three nights, the Dashboard says Needs attention.

Kord öösel, alates kella 03.30-st, saadab pood Campaign Intelligence'ile oma toodete nimekirja koos teabega, kas iga toode on laos. Campaign Intelligence lõpetab nimekirjast välja jäänud toote soovitamise, nii et kohale jõudmata kustutamine või laoseisu muutus saab öö jooksul parandatud.

  • Pood, kus on üle 50 000 tooterea, nimekirja ei saada ja logi ütleb seda igal ööl. Tavalised toodete muudatused lähevad ikka.
  • Kui paigalduse kaks poodi on ühendatud samasse Campaign Intelligence'i poodi, nende kohta nimekirja ei saadeta ja töölaud ütleb seda: kustutatud toode või laoseisu muutus jõuab Campaign Intelligence'i alles toote järgmise muudatusega.
  • Kui nimekirja pole kolm ööd saadetud, ütleb töölaud Vajab tähelepanu.

Smaily Connect saadab kontakte, automaatikate e-kirju ja Campaign Intelligence'i andmeid taustal. PrestaShopil pole ajastajat, seega käivitab töö cron-rida.

Reading the cardKaardi lugemine

Adding the cron lineCron-rea lisamine

  1. Copy the cron line from the Background tasks card. While the state is Visitors only or Not running, the card shows the line; while it is Running, click Show the cron line. Only an administrator who may change the Smaily Connect settings sees the line and the button.
  2. Add the line to the crontab of your server, for example in the cron jobs of your hosting control panel. A cron service that calls an address for you can call the line's address instead.

The line calls one address of your store every minute, with a secret token. It looks like this, with your own address and token:

* * * * * curl -fsS "https://shop.example.com/module/smailyforprestashop/tick?token=<token>" > /dev/null

One cron line runs every task. Keep the token to yourself: a request without the right token is refused.

  1. Kopeeri cron-rida kaardilt Taustatööd. Kui olek on Ainult külastustest või Ei tööta, näitab kaart rida; kui olek on Töötab, klõpsa Näita cron-rida. Rida ja nuppu näeb ainult administraator, kellel on õigus Smaily Connecti seadeid muuta.
  2. Lisa rida oma serveri crontabi, näiteks oma majutuse juhtpaneeli cron-tööde hulka. Rea aadressi võib selle asemel välja kutsuda ka cron-teenus, mis aadresse sinu eest kutsub.

Rida kutsub iga minut välja sinu poe ühe aadressi koos salajase võtmega. See näeb välja nii, sinu enda aadressi ja võtmega:

* * * * * curl -fsS "https://shop.example.com/module/smailyforprestashop/tick?token=<token>" > /dev/null

Üks cron-rida käivitab kõik tööd. Hoia võti enda teada: õige võtmeta päring lükatakse tagasi.

The cron URLs of version 2.xVersiooni 2.x cron-URL-id

The two cron URLs of version 2.x — the contact synchronization URL (SmailyCustomerCron) and the abandoned cart URL (SmailyCartCron) — keep working with their tokens. Each now runs every task, so an upgraded store keeps sending without a change to its crontab. The one cron line of the card replaces both; you can then remove the two old lines.

A 2.x cron URL that had no token could be called by anyone. The upgrade gives such a URL a new token, so its old crontab line no longer works, and a notice on the Smaily Connect pages says so: use the cron line of the card instead.

Versiooni 2.x kaks cron-URL-i — kontaktide sünkroonimise URL (SmailyCustomerCron) ja hüljatud ostukorvi URL (SmailyCartCron) — töötavad oma võtmetega edasi. Kumbki käivitab nüüd kõik tööd, nii et uuendatud pood saadab edasi ilma crontabi muutmata. Kaardi üks cron-rida asendab mõlemad; seejärel võid kaks vana rida eemaldada.

Võtmeta 2.x cron-URL-i sai igaüks välja kutsuda. Uuendamine annab sellisele URL-ile uue võtme, nii et selle vana crontabi rida enam ei tööta, ja Smaily Connecti lehtede teade ütleb seda: kasuta selle asemel kaardi cron-rida.

Failed rows and retryEbaõnnestunud read ja kordus

  • Automatic retry. A delivery that fails for a passing reason is tried again after 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes — 5 attempts in all — before it is marked Failed.
  • No retry. A delivery that was refused outright — wrong credentials, a deleted workflow, data Smaily or Campaign Intelligence calls invalid — is marked Failed at once, so you see it now.
  • The banner. When rows failed in the last 24 hours, a banner above the list counts them, and Show failed (24h) lists them.
  • Retry — tick failed rows and click Retry; Select all retries every failed row that the filter lists. A row is left alone when sending it again would not be safe, for example when a later email of the same kind already reached the contact, and the answer says how many rows were skipped.
  • Send again — on one failed row, queues a new attempt as a new row; the failed row stays as your record.

Fix the cause first (see the tables above), then retry.

  • Automaatne kordus. Möödaminevatel põhjustel ebaõnnestunud saadetist proovitakse uuesti 1, 5, 15 ja 60 minuti pärast — kokku 5 katset —, enne kui see märgitakse olekuga Ebaõnnestunud.
  • Kordust ei ole. Otse tagasi lükatud saadetis — valed kasutajaandmed, kustutatud töövoog, andmed, mida Smaily või Campaign Intelligence peab vigaseks — märgitakse kohe olekuga Ebaõnnestunud, et näeksid seda kohe.
  • Bänner. Kui viimase 24 tunni jooksul ebaõnnestus ridu, loendab bänner loendi kohal need ja Näita ebaõnnestunuid (24 h) loetleb need.
  • Proovi uuesti — märgi ebaõnnestunud read ja klõpsa Proovi uuesti; Vali kõik (Select all) proovib uuesti iga ebaõnnestunud rida, mida filter loetleb. Rida jäetakse puutumata, kui selle uuesti saatmine poleks ohutu, näiteks kui sama liiki hilisem e-kiri on kontaktini juba jõudnud, ja vastus ütleb, mitu rida vahele jäeti.
  • Saada uuesti — ühel ebaõnnestunud real paneb uue katse järjekorda uue reana; ebaõnnestunud rida jääb sinu kirjeks.

Paranda kõigepealt põhjus (vt ülaltoodud tabeleid) ja proovi siis uuesti.

Nothing is syncingMidagi ei sünkroonita

  2. Is Smaily connected? The Dashboard says Not connected when Smaily has not accepted the saved credentials.
  3. Is contact sync on? Sync contacts to Smaily on Settings > Contacts, for the right shop. On a new install, contacts sync only once the Contacts step is saved. Under Checkout opt-in only, only checkout signups sync.
  4. What does the Log say? Filter on Failed and read Last Error — see The Log, error messages & fixes.
  1. Kas taustatööd töötavad? Töölaua kaart Taustatööd ütleb Ei tööta, kui need pole 15 minutit käivitunud — vt Cron-rea lisamine.
  2. Kas Smaily on ühendatud? Töölaud ütleb Pole ühendatud, kui Smaily pole salvestatud kasutajaandmeid vastu võtnud.

A contact unsubscribed in Smaily but is still subscribed in PrestaShopKontakt loobus Smailys, kuid on PrestaShopis ikka tellija

Unsubscribes come back from Smaily every 15 minutes, and only under Subscribers only (consent), while the background tasks run. Under the other two modes, Smaily keeps its own unsubscribe and PrestaShop is not changed.

Loobumised jõuavad Smailyst tagasi iga 15 minuti järel ja ainult valiku Ainult turunduskirjade tellijad (nõusolekuga) korral, kui taustatööd töötavad. Kahe teise režiimi korral jääb loobumine Smailysse ja PrestaShopi ei muudeta.

The abandoned-cart email never arrivesHüljatud ostukorvi kiri ei jõua kohale

  • The automation is Enabled with a workflow picked, for the cart's shop (and language).
  • The cart has an email address: a signed-in customer's, or one typed at checkout.
  • The cart has been idle longer than Wait (minutes), but less than 24 hours, and the background tasks run.
  • The cart was not reminded already, and the address got no reminder for another cart in the last 24 hours.
  • The consent mode allows a reminder to this shopper, and the address has not unsubscribed in Smaily — a skipped cart's row in the Log reads Skipped with the reason.
  • Automaatika on Lubatud ja töövoog on valitud, ostukorvi poe (ja keele) jaoks.
  • Ostukorvil on e-posti aadress: sisse loginud kliendi oma või kassas sisestatud aadress.
  • Ostukorv on olnud tegevuseta kauem kui Ooteaeg (minutites), kuid vähem kui 24 tundi, ja taustatööd töötavad.
  • Ostukorvile pole juba meeldetuletust saadetud ja aadress pole viimase 24 tunni jooksul saanud teise ostukorvi meeldetuletust.
  • Nõusolekurežiim lubab sellele ostjale meeldetuletust ja aadress pole Smailys loobunud — vahele jäetud ostukorvi rida logis näitab olekut Vahele jäetud koos põhjusega.

Where is the checkout newsletter checkbox?Kus on kassa uudiskirja märkeruut?

How do I disconnect Campaign Intelligence?Kuidas Campaign Intelligence'i lahti ühendada?

Press Disconnect under Settings > Intelligence and confirm. The module stops sending data and tells Campaign Intelligence that this connection ends. If your store has no other connection, Campaign Intelligence pauses it: no recommendations and none of its automated emails until you connect again. Your data there is kept. Connecting again needs a new setup link from Smaily. To stop Smaily syncing, switch off Sync contacts to Smaily and the automations.

Vajuta jaotises Seaded > Intelligence Katkesta ühendus ja kinnita. Moodul lõpetab andmete saatmise ja teatab Campaign Intelligence'ile, et see ühendus lõpeb. Kui sinu poel teist ühendust ei ole, peatab Campaign Intelligence selle: soovitusi ega selle automaatseid e-kirju ei tule, kuni ühendad uuesti. Sinu andmed seal säilivad. Does disabling or removing the module delete my data?Kas mooduli keelamine või eemaldamine kustutab andmed?

  • Disabling the module in Modules > Module Manager stops it; its settings and data stay.
  • Uninstalling removes all module data: every setting, the cron tokens, the module's database tables (the queues, the Log, the cart reminders) and its cache files. Reset is an uninstall and an install, so it removes them too. Never uninstall to upgrade.
  • Nothing is deleted in Smaily or Campaign Intelligence. Contacts and data already there stay. On uninstall, as on disconnect, the module tells Campaign Intelligence that the connection ends. The discount codes the module made stay in Catalog > Discounts.
  • Mooduli keelamine menüüs Moodulid > Moodulihaldur peatab selle; selle seaded ja andmed jäävad alles.
  • Eemaldamine kustutab kõik mooduli andmed: iga seade, cron-võtmed, mooduli andmebaasi tabelid (järjekorrad, logi, ostukorvide meeldetuletused) ja vahemälufailid. Lähtesta (Reset) on eemaldamine ja paigaldus, seega kustutab ka see need. Ära kunagi eemalda moodulit selle uuendamiseks.
  • Smailys ega Campaign Intelligence'is ei kustutata midagi. Sinna juba jõudnud kontaktid ja andmed jäävad. Eemaldamisel, nagu ka lahtiühendamisel, teatab moodul Campaign Intelligence'ile, et ühendus lõpeb. Mooduli loodud sooduskoodid jäävad menüüsse Kataloog > Allahindlused.

How do I know a newer version is out?Kust ma tean, et uuem versioon on väljas?

When a newer version of the module is out, the Smaily Connect pages say so, with links to download it and to read what is new. The module asks GitHub for the latest version once a day and sends no store data. To update, upload the new zip in Modules > Module Manager as on the first install; never uninstall first.

Kui mooduli uuem versioon on väljas, ütlevad Smaily Connecti lehed seda koos linkidega selle allalaadimiseks ja uuenduste lugemiseks. Moodul küsib GitHubilt uusimat versiooni kord päevas ega saada poe andmeid. Uuendamiseks laadi uus ZIP menüüs Moodulid > Moodulihaldur üles nagu esmasel paigaldusel; ära kunagi eemalda moodulit enne.

Which languages does the module speak?Mis keeli moodul räägib?

Personalization opt-outPersonaliseerimisest loobumine

In a store that is connected to Campaign Intelligence, a signed-in customer finds Personalization in their account. The page Personalized Recommendations has one checkbox, Allow personalized recommendations, and the button Save Preference. A store without a Campaign Intelligence connection has no such link and no such page.

  • A customer who unticks the box is left out of personalized recommendations; the newsletters then show generic content. The customer can tick it again at any time, and the last choice counts.
  • A customer who unsubscribes from the newsletter is also left out, and is included again after subscribing again, unless they had unticked the box themselves.
  • The choice is kept by the store and sent to Campaign Intelligence as a queued delivery, so an outage only delays it. An opted-out shopper's browsing is not linked to them.

Campaign Intelligence'iga ühendatud poes leiab sisse loginud klient oma kontolt lehe Personaliseerimine. Lehel Personaalsed soovitused on üks märkeruut, Luba personaalsed soovitused, ja nupp Salvesta eelistus. Campaign Intelligence'i ühenduseta poes sellist linki ega lehte ei ole.

  • Klient, kes märke eemaldab, jäetakse personaalsetest soovitustest välja; uudiskirjad näitavad siis üldist sisu. Klient võib märke igal ajal tagasi panna ja kehtib viimane valik.
  • Uudiskirjast loobunud klient jäetakse samuti välja ja ta kaasatakse uuesti pärast uuesti liitumist, kui ta polnud märget ise eemaldanud.
  • Valikut hoiab pood ja see saadetakse Campaign Intelligence'ile järjekorras saadetisena, nii et katkestus ainult viivitab seda. Loobunud ostja sirvimist temaga ei seota.

Export and erasure (GDPR)Eksport ja kustutamine (GDPR)

A customer can ask for a copy of their personal data, or to have it erased. Smaily Connect answers both for the data that the module itself keeps, and for the customer's data in Campaign Intelligence.

With PrestaShop's Official GDPR compliance module (psgdpr) the module needs no setup: that module's export and deletion include Smaily Connect. On PrestaShop 9, that module's own admin screen (version 1.4.3, as PrestaShop 9.2.0 ships it) cannot find or show a customer; use the form below there.

On the Log page, with or without that module, the form Privacy request is under the list, for an employee who may change the Log:

  1. Type the customer's Email address.
  2. Export saves a file with what the module holds about that address, and nothing of anybody else.
  3. Erase asks for confirmation and then erases it, in every shop. It cannot be undone.

What an erasure does:

  • Messages still waiting to be sent are deleted.
  • Rows of the Log that were already sent, skipped or failed stay as a record, without the contact: their Entity reads [erased], the data sent and the answer are removed, and they cannot be sent again.
  • The address is taken off the customer's cart reminders, and a cart about to be reminded gets no reminder.
  • The email click and the visitor token kept with the customer's carts are deleted. An order keeps the email click that led to it, without the visitor token.
  • The discount codes the module issued to the customer that no order used are removed. A used code stays with its order.
  • Campaign Intelligence is asked to delete the customer; the request waits in the Log as engine.customer_delete and is retried until Campaign Intelligence answers.
  • The customer's choice about personalization is kept, without the address, so that an opt-out still holds if the same address orders again.

The form handles the module's data only. The customer account, the orders and the Smaily contact stay: delete or anonymize the account in PrestaShop, and the contact in Smaily.

Klient võib küsida oma isikuandmete koopiat või nende kustutamist. Smaily Connect vastab mõlemale nende andmete osas, mida moodul ise hoiab, ja kliendi andmete osas Campaign Intelligence'is.

PrestaShopi ametliku GDPR-i vastavusmooduliga (psgdpr) ei vaja moodul seadistamist: selle mooduli eksport ja kustutamine hõlmavad Smaily Connecti. PrestaShop 9-s ei leia ega näita selle mooduli enda halduskuva (versioon 1.4.3, nagu PrestaShop 9.2.0 seda tarnib) klienti; kasuta seal allolevat vormi.

Lehel Logi, selle mooduliga või ilma, on loendi all vorm Isikuandmete päring töötajale, kellel on õigus logi muuta:

  1. Sisesta kliendi E-posti aadress.
  2. Ekspordi salvestab faili sellega, mida moodul selle aadressi kohta hoiab, ja mitte midagi kellegi teise kohta.
  3. Kustuta küsib kinnitust ja kustutab need seejärel kõigis poodides. Seda ei saa tagasi võtta.

Mida kustutamine teeb:

  • Saatmist ootavad sõnumid kustutatakse.
  • Juba saadetud, vahele jäetud või ebaõnnestunud logiread jäävad kirjeks, ilma kontaktita: nende Kirje on [erased], saadetud andmed ja vastus eemaldatakse ning neid ei saa uuesti saata.
  • Aadress eemaldatakse kliendi ostukorvide meeldetuletustest ja ostukorv, mis oli meeldetuletust saamas, seda ei saa.
  • Kliendi ostukorvide juures hoitav e-kirja klõps ja külastajatunnus kustutatakse. Tellimus jätab alles e-kirja klõpsu, mis selleni viis, ilma külastajatunnuseta.
  • Mooduli poolt kliendile antud sooduskoodid, mida ükski tellimus ei kasutanud, eemaldatakse. Kasutatud kood jääb oma tellimuse juurde.
  • Campaign Intelligence'il palutakse klient kustutada; päring ootab logis kui engine.customer_delete ja seda proovitakse uuesti, kuni Campaign Intelligence vastab.
  • Kliendi personaliseerimise valik jääb alles, ilma aadressita, et loobumine kehtiks ka siis, kui sama aadress uuesti tellib.

Vorm käsitleb ainult mooduli andmeid. Kliendikonto, tellimused ja Smaily kontakt jäävad: kustuta või anonüümi konto PrestaShopis ja kontakt Smailys.

Upgrading from Smaily for PrestaShop 2.xUuendamine versioonilt Smaily for PrestaShop 2.x

Smaily Connect 3 is the same module as Smaily for PrestaShop 2.x — the folder smailyforprestashop stays — so it upgrades in place:

  1. Download the zip of the new release, open Modules > Module Manager, click Upload a module and drop the zip there. Do not uninstall the old version first: uninstalling removes every setting of the module.
  2. Open Customers > Smaily Connect. Notices at the top of the Smaily Connect pages tell what the upgrade changed and what it left switched off, with the reason. Close each notice once you have read it; it then goes away for every administrator. Until then the other back-office pages show one line with a link to the Dashboard.
  3. If the upgrade was interrupted, the pages show The upgrade to Smaily Connect 3.0.0 is not finished with the button Finish upgrade. Smaily Connect stays paused and your previous settings stay in place until the upgrade finishes; it also finishes on its own with the next run of a cron URL.

What is carried over. The Smaily account, contact synchronization, the opt-in and abandoned cart automations with their workflows, the RSS defaults, and the record of the carts that were already reminded — a cart that 2.x reminded is not reminded again. Your cron URLs and your RSS feed URLs keep working. The initial setup of an upgraded store is filled in with the carried-over settings and is not required: every page is open at once.

What changes.

  • The consent mode starts at Subscribers only (consent) with Include guests on, the narrowest mode that matches 2.x. The first check of each Smaily account takes only unsubscribes, and marks nobody subscribed again. The abandoned-cart reminder now follows the consent mode, so a store may send fewer reminders than 2.x did; the Log shows each cart that got none as skipped.
  • The abandoned-cart reminder always carries all its fields; the field picker is gone. A 2.x waiting time longer than 1435 minutes becomes 1435 minutes, and a notice says so.
  • A 2.x cron URL without a token gets a new token (see The cron URLs of 2.x). One cron line now runs every task.
  • Browse tracking and recommendations start off, until you confirm your cookie banner.
  • The module no longer installs an override of the newsletter module; the upgrade removes the one 2.x installed. Files of the old version that 3.0.0 does not use are deleted from the module's folder; a file that cannot be deleted is named in a notice, so that you can delete it by hand.

Smaily Connect 3 on sama moodul mis Smaily for PrestaShop 2.x — kaust smailyforprestashop jääb —, nii et see uueneb kohapeal:

  1. Laadi alla uue väljalaske ZIP, ava Moodulid > Moodulihaldur, klõpsa Laadi moodul üles (Upload a module) ja lohista ZIP sinna. Ära eemalda vana versiooni enne: eemaldamine kustutab kõik mooduli seaded.
